COMPANY INFORMATION AND FAQs - Sample Startup Pvt Ltd

1. About the Company
Sample Startup Pvt Ltd is a software startup with about 40 employees, founded in 2022, building web and mobile products for clients.

2. When is salary paid?
Salary is credited on the last working day of each month.

3. What documents are needed on the first day?
New joiners should bring ID proof, address proof, educational certificates, relieving letter from the previous employer, and bank account details.

4. What is the onboarding process?
HR conducts an orientation on day 1. Employees receive a laptop and email account, and are assigned a buddy for the first month.

5. When do I get my payslip?
Payslips are shared by email within 3 days of salary credit.

6. How is the probation review done?
The manager reviews performance at the end of the probation period and informs HR, who issues the confirmation letter.
