LEAVE POLICY - Sample Startup Pvt Ltd

1. Casual Leave
Every employee gets 12 casual leaves per year. Casual leave cannot be carried forward to the next year.

2. Sick Leave
Every employee gets 6 sick leaves per year. A medical certificate is required if sick leave is more than 2 consecutive days.

3. Earned Leave
Employees who complete 1 year of service get 15 earned leaves per year. Up to 10 earned leaves can be carried forward to the next year.

4. Maternity and Paternity Leave
Maternity leave is given as per applicable law. Paternity leave is 5 working days, to be taken within 1 month of the child's birth.

5. How to Apply
Leave must be requested at least 2 days in advance by email to the reporting manager. Emergency leave can be informed by phone on the same day, and the email must follow within 24 hours.

6. Unpaid Leave
Leave taken beyond the available balance is treated as unpaid leave, and the salary is reduced for those days.

7. Leave During Probation
Employees on probation get casual and sick leave but cannot take earned leave.
