NOTICE PERIOD AND RESIGNATION POLICY - Sample Startup Pvt Ltd

1. Probation Period
The probation period is 3 months from the date of joining. It may be extended by 1 month on the manager's recommendation.

2. Notice Period During Probation
An employee on probation must give 15 days notice to resign.

3. Notice Period After Confirmation
A confirmed employee must give 60 days notice to resign. The company may also give 60 days notice or pay in lieu of notice to terminate employment.

4. How to Resign
Resignation must be submitted in writing by email to the reporting manager, with HR in copy. The notice period starts from the date the resignation is accepted by HR.

5. Notice Period Buyout
An employee may request an early release by paying the salary for the remaining notice days. Approval is at the company's discretion.

6. Exit Process
Before the last working day the employee must return all company assets, complete knowledge transfer, and attend an exit interview with HR.

7. Full and Final Settlement
Pending salary, leave encashment (as per policy) and other dues are settled within 45 days of the last working day.
