REIMBURSEMENT POLICY - Sample Startup Pvt Ltd

1. Eligible Expenses
The company reimburses business travel, client meeting expenses, and approved work-related purchases. Personal expenses are not reimbursed.

2. Travel Reimbursement
Local travel for client meetings is reimbursed at actual cost for auto, taxi or bus. Travel by personal vehicle is reimbursed at Rs. 8 per km. Outstation travel must be approved in advance.

3. Internet Allowance
Employees approved for work from home can claim up to Rs. 500 per month for internet charges, with the bill attached.

4. Meal Expenses
Meals during client visits or outstation travel are reimbursed up to Rs. 400 per meal, with a bill.

5. How to Claim
Submit the claim form by email to HR with original bills or receipts, approved by the reporting manager. Claims without bills are not paid.

6. Claim Deadline
Claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense. Late claims may be rejected.

7. Payment
Approved claims are paid along with the next month's salary.
