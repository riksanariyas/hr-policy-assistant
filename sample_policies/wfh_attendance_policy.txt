WORK HOURS, ATTENDANCE AND WORK FROM HOME POLICY - Sample Startup Pvt Ltd

1. Office Working Hours
Office hours are 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM, Monday to Friday, with a 1-hour lunch break. The 1st and 3rd Saturdays of every month are working days, from 9:30 AM to 1:30 PM.

2. Attendance Marking
Attendance is marked using the biometric device at the office. Employees working from home must log in to the team chat by 9:45 AM and log out by 6:30 PM.

3. Late Arrival
Arrival after 9:45 AM is marked as a late mark. Three late marks in a month are counted as one half-day of casual leave.

4. Half Day
Working fewer than 4 hours in a day is marked as an absence. Working between 4 and 8 hours is marked as a half-day.

5. Work From Home Eligibility
Employees who have completed probation can work from home up to 2 days per week, with the reporting manager's approval. Employees on probation work from the office.

6. Requesting Work From Home
WFH must be requested by email to the reporting manager at least 1 day in advance. Permanent remote work is not available.

7. Overtime
Overtime work must be approved in advance by the manager. Eligibility and rates are as per applicable law and the employment contract.
